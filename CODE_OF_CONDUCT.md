# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(v2.1) as its code of conduct.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep discussion
focused on the work and the project's [invariants](CONTRIBUTING.md). Harassment or
demeaning conduct is not tolerated.

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately to **imjhyuk@gmail.com**. As a
single-maintainer project, reports are reviewed and acted on a best-effort basis.
